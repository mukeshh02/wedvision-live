Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

WedVision ("we", "us", "our") builds custom software, websites, client portals, and studio management platforms for wedding industry professionals, including photographers, filmmakers, planners, emcees, and wedding decorators. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, book a consultation, or partner with us to develop custom software, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Your name, business name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and location.
  • Business Requirements: Details regarding your current wedding workflows, team structure, client management challenges, and project goals.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser types, and anonymous interaction metrics to improve site performance.

3. How We Use Information

We use your data solely to communicate with you regarding your inquiries, plan and deliver custom software solutions, provide post-deployment support, and maintain platform security. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Client Data & Custom Platforms

For custom systems, client galleries, or CRM platforms built for our studio partners, data collected through your specific platform is owned entirely by you. We adhere to strict confidentiality protocols and role-based access security.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and secure hosting infrastructure. We retain contact inquiries only for as long as needed to fulfill our business relationship and legal obligations.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data updates, please reach out to us directly via our consultation channel or email us at hello@wedvision.in.
