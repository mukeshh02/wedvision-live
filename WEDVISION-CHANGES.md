# WedVision visual-story update

## Visual direction

Replaced the green presentation with warm white, near-black and the supplied logo’s magenta. Existing local font families and the original logo artwork are retained.

## What changed

- Hero: “Your wedding business. Your custom software.” with a connected wedding-workspace concept and floating file, team and inquiry cards.
- Visual comparison: scattered messages, folders and follow-ups beside an organized custom workflow.
- Six service cards now show miniature client records, project stages, file sharing, team assignments, a portfolio concept and a custom-workflow diagram.
- Four process cards illustrate meeting, planning, building and support.
- Six audience cards use recognizable profession symbols.
- Contact section and footer use the new palette; supplied landscape wordmark appears on the dark contact panel.
- Existing #tools and #who-for URLs still work as anchor aliases.
- Legal-page shells use the same visual design. No legal wording was invented.

## Motion and accessibility

Scroll reveals, staggered connection nodes, floating cards, animated connector lines and restrained hover movement. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Keyboard focus styles, descriptive navigation labels and decorative-scene accessibility labels are included. Primary content is visible without JavaScript.

## Content boundaries

Examples are labeled as illustrative custom solutions. No invented customer names, results, testimonials, certifications or pricing. Meeting and social destinations remain TODO placeholders.
